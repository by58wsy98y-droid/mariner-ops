# Retraining the throttles

Teaches the control box where the lever sits at idle and full, ahead and astern. Do it after any
control module (ERIM/CSIM) change, and any time idle or full doesn't match the lever.

**Engine OFF, key ON. Tied up with bow, stern and spring lines. Two people.**

| # | Do this | You'll see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Push the red ACTIVE button once | Red light on |
| 2 | Lever to the **first notch ahead (forward idle)** | — |
| 3 | Push LO IDLE and **hold it** | ~2 s later green light flashes |
| 4 | Still holding, tap the red ACTIVE once. Let go of both | Green stops flashing — reading saved |
| 5 | Lever **all the way ahead** | — |
| 6–7 | Same hold / tap / let go | Green flashes, then stops |
| 8 | Lever back through neutral to the **first notch astern** | — |
| 9–10 | Same hold / tap / let go | Green flashes, then stops |
| 11 | Lever **all the way back** | — |
| 12–13 | Same hold / tap / let go | Green flashes, then stops |
| 14 | Lever to neutral. Key OFF. Count to 5. Key ON | Panel restarts |
| 15 | Repeat on the other engine's lever, then at every other station | — |

**Check (captain at the sticks; boat pushes on the lines):** start, lever in neutral, tap ACTIVE;
slowly sweep to full ahead and back, full astern and back. Smooth, no codes, idle returns = done.
Otherwise redo that lever.

Dead band option: push the lever a hair past the idle notch before holding LO IDLE at the two idle
positions. Narrow range. Skip unless asked.

**What it does NOT do:** fix a bad wire or ground; change the ECM's own throttle window or fault
response (those are EMDEC programming, laptop at the diagnostic connector).

Log it: date, engine, station, who, dead band or not, result of the check.
