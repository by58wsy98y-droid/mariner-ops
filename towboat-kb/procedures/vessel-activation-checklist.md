# Vessel activation (engineering)

Outlines the process for bringing vessels in/out of a standby status. Reference: Vessel Operations Manual 2.4.
Going in: [Vessel deactivation checklist](vessel-deactivation-checklist.md).

- [ ] Verify outstanding deficiencies have been resolved
- [ ] Uncover: main engine exhaust stacks, main engine air intakes, engine room fan intake and exhaust vents
- [ ] Inventory cleaning supplies, tools, filters, and critical spares and order necessary items
- [ ] Conduct a security sweep of the engine room
- [ ] Test: Fire Pump, Emergency Fuel Shutoff, Machinery Alarms System, High Fuel Level Alarm System
- [ ] If safe to do so before leaving mooring, have wheelman on watch verify throttle engagement ahead and astern for all main engines and swing steering and flanking rudders
- [ ] Sound all fuel tanks, potable water, main engine lube oil, slop oil, generator oil and inform Wheelman on watch of any need to put orders in.
- [ ] Assist wheelman on watch with any other pre-departure machinery checks
- [ ] Inform your Port Engineer of vessels activation status to resume normal Ripple Checklist timelines
