# Fuel Centrifuge Maintenance — Quarterly

## Step 1 — Preparation & Safety

### Parts required
As required for unit inspection.

### Tools / consumables required
- Rags, oil pads, flashlight
- Grease
- General hand tools

### Safety notes / cautions / warnings
**WARNING:** The fuel oil separator (centrifuge) has numerous sources of energy. These energy sources shall be considered before undertaking a maintenance task. Sources of energy shall be secured through the appropriate LOTO guidance as needed.

**CAUTION:** Pay special attention to valve arrangements and ensure unit is lined up properly before, during and after task.

**IMPORTANT:** Allow at least 0.5 hours to complete this task.

## Step 2 — Quarterly Centrifuge Service

### 1. Gather Tools
Gather the necessary tools needed to complete maintenance.

### 2. Permit-to-Work / GAR SAFE
As necessary, complete a Permit-to-Work with all persons involved and discuss the need for additional measures such as Perimeter Security, Fall-Arrest Protection and Equipment Lockout / Tagout.

### 3. Secure Unit via LOTO
Secure the unit via lock out / tag out protocol.

If unit fails to attain full speed within 2-3 minutes, the element shall be changed.

### 4. Pull Motor and Clutch Assembly
Pull motor and clutch assembly.

a. Inspect the friction elements.

i. Replace elements if excessive wear or damage is observed.

b. Inspect clutch drum.

i. Check for excessive wear and / or damage.

ii. If issues with hub are observed, discuss findings with Port Engineer.

### 5. Re-install Motor and Clutch
Re-install motor and clutch assembly.

### 6. Lubricate Bearings (if equipped)
If unit is equipped with grease fittings (non-sealed bearings):

a. Lubricate bearings with a high-speed bearing grease.

### 7. Reinstate Unit
Reinstate unit.
